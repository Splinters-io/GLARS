Multi-Jurisdiction Data Journey

Visualizing the complex interplay of legal frameworks in cross-border data flows

UK

UK TLD (.uk domain)

Legal Registration Jurisdiction

A .uk domain places the domain itself under UK law and the UK registry, Nominet. On its own it gives UK authorities no access to data hosted elsewhere, but it signals a UK-facing service, which brings the operator closer to the reach of UK investigatory powers.

UK Government Agencies with Legal Authority

  • GCHQ (Government Communications Headquarters)
  • MI5 (Security Service)
  • Home Office
  • National Crime Agency
Investigatory Powers Act 2016High
Interception, equipment interference and bulk powers, with warrants approved by a Judicial Commissioner. Technical capability notices can require providers to maintain access capabilities. Amended in 2024.
Domain suspensionMedium
Nominet can suspend .uk domains at the request of UK law enforcement, taking the service offline without touching the data itself.
US

US Corporate Jurisdiction

Service Provider & Corporate Control

The US-based company operating the service creates a powerful secondary jurisdiction through corporate control, regardless of server location.

US Government Agencies with Legal Authority

  • FBI (Federal Bureau of Investigation)
  • NSA (National Security Agency)
  • DOJ (Department of Justice)
  • FISC (Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Court)
  • DHS (Department of Homeland Security)
CLOUD ActHigh
Allows DOJ and FBI to compel US companies to provide data regardless of where it's stored.
FISA Section 702High
Targeting of non-US persons abroad with compelled help from US providers. The statutory authority lapsed in June 2026, but collection continues under court certifications approved in March 2026.
National Security LettersHigh
Enables FBI to issue secret demands for data with gag orders, limiting transparency.
Executive Order 12333Medium
Authorizes NSA signals intelligence collection outside the US, with oversight largely within the executive branch.
IN

India Data Storage

Physical Server Location

Physical data storage in India creates a third legal jurisdiction with its own access powers, regardless of corporate control.

Indian Government Agencies with Legal Authority

  • CERT-In (Indian Computer Emergency Response Team)
  • Intelligence Bureau
  • NATGRID (National Intelligence Grid)
  • Reserve Bank of India
  • Ministry of Electronics and IT
Information Technology ActHigh
Section 69 lets the government direct interception, monitoring or decryption of information by authorised agencies such as the Intelligence Bureau. Intermediaries must assist.
Data Localization RequirementsMedium
The Reserve Bank of India requires payment system data to be stored only in India.
Telecommunications Act 2023Medium
Section 20 and the 2024 interception rules let the Union Home Secretary order interception of messages, reviewed by a committee of senior officials.
Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023Medium
Rules notified in November 2025 phase in until May 2027. The government can exempt its own agencies on security grounds and require organisations to provide information.

Jurisdiction Interactions

These three jurisdictions don't operate in isolation - they interact in ways that can amplify risk:

UK-US Data Access AgreementHigh
In force since October 2022 under the CLOUD Act. Each country's authorities can serve orders for serious-crime investigations directly on the other's providers.
Five Eyes intelligence sharingHigh
Under the UKUSA arrangements GCHQ and the NSA share signals intelligence, so data collected by one can reach the other.
Corporate Control LeverageHigh
US authorities can compel a US parent company to produce data held by its Indian operations.
Jurisdictional ConflictsMedium
A US order to produce data held in India may conflict with Indian law, leaving the provider unable to comply with both.
